# Competition Requirements

Verified against the live pages and forms on July 30, 2026. Requirements can change; recheck immediately before submission.

## 1. TechRise

Application: https://www.techrise.co/pitch-your-startup

### Fit and timing

- Rolling application for Chicagoland founders.
- The profile is considered for multiple TechRise competitions.
- Upcoming listed competitions include Emerging Tech (AI & Quantum) on August 20, South Chicago on September 19, and Founders Week at UIUC on October 1.
- Selected founders receive coaching and mentorship before a live pitch.
- Non-dilutive capital is awarded at events.
- No application fee was shown.

### Application requirements

The live form is a common Chicago startup ecosystem profile. Some profile information may be displayed publicly in a founder gallery.

Required or materially important fields:

- Consent to the public founder profile
- Founder name, email, title, and company
- Company website and description
- Product status: pre-product, live beta, in-market pre-revenue, revenue-generating, scaling, or other
- Product type: software/experience, service, or physical product
- Funding stage
- Founded date and startup LinkedIn
- Concrete problem
- Solution and differentiation
- Revenue model
- Target market and customer acquisition
- Competitors
- “Why you, why now?”
- Progress demonstrating viability
- Revenue range and capital raised
- Last dilutive raise, next raise timing, and target amount
- Current business needs
- Pitch deck and video-pitch links, if available
- Address and Chicago/Illinois connection
- Cofounder status and team size

### SpotLift blockers

- Exact Chicago/Illinois connection.
- Approved traction, revenue, capital-raised, and fundraising answers.
- Public company website and preferably company LinkedIn.
- A shareable deck makes the application materially stronger.

## 2. South Side Pitch

Application: https://ij.org/ij-clinic-on-entrepreneurship/south-side-pitch/apply-to-south-side-pitch/south-side-pitch-application/

Deadline: August 7, 2026.

Final: October 15, 2026, in Chicago.

Prize pool: more than $40,000; first place $15,000.

### Hard eligibility

All applicable requirements must be confirmed:

- For-profit.
- Fewer than 50 employees.
- Under $1 million in 2025 revenue.
- Not a franchise.
- Not a prior South Side Pitch finalist.
- Not a current or former IJ Clinic client.
- Business is headquartered inside Chicago and south of Roosevelt Road. If home-based or without a physical business location, the founder’s residence must meet the same boundary.
- Past idea stage: working prototype, customer, or active pilot.
- Pitching founder is an owner/founder, age 18+, an Illinois resident currently located in Illinois, and not disqualified by an IJ relationship.
- Founder must have a meaningful connection to the South Side.

### Exact application content

Business facts:

- Legal business name, website, 2025 revenue rounded to the nearest $10,000, years in business, and business address.
- Industry.
- How the applicant heard about the competition.

Essays:

- Product/service description: 1,000 characters, no more than eight sentences.
- How the business is rooted in the South Side: 1,000 characters.
- Business impact on the South Side: 1,000 characters.
- Entrepreneurial journey and origin story: 1,000 characters.
- How creativity and innovation helped the business grow or overcome challenges: 1,000 characters.
- Differentiation: 1,000 characters.
- Awards, press, and programs: 500 characters.
- One or two recent actions taken to strengthen long-term growth: 1,000 characters.
- Use of the $15,000 first-place prize: 1,000 characters.
- Optional regulatory barriers: 1,000 characters.

Later stages:

- Approximately 25 semifinalists submit a 90-second original video by September 4.
- At least 30 seconds must show the founder’s face and the founder’s voice must be the main audio.
- Five finalists give a three-minute in-person pitch without PowerPoint and answer questions.

### Special content rule

The published rules state that submissions must be original and contain “no AI.” The founder must personally author or substantially rewrite all submitted language. This workspace may be used as a planning outline and editing checklist, not pasted verbatim into the application.

## 3. Slush Startup Stage

Application: https://slush.typeform.com/to/ZZtRWeVd

Deadline: August 31, 2026.

Event: November 18–19, 2026, Helsinki.

### Eligibility and format

- Pre-seed through Series A.
- Startup founded no earlier than 2022.
- Sixty founders are selected for ten-minute pitches or live demos.
- No application fee was shown, but attending Slush requires a pass and travel.
- The startup pass was publicly listed at €395 including VAT when checked.

### Live form questions

- Founder name, title, email, and LinkedIn.
- Startup name and official website/LinkedIn.
- Optional Dealroom link.
- Country.
- Founding year.
- Stage: pre-seed, seed, Series A, or other.
- Industry.
- Why the founder should speak on the Startup Stage.
- What makes the startup especially relevant this year: maximum 400 characters.
- Whether the company plans to announce anything on stage.
- Contact email.

### SpotLift blockers

- Founder biography and LinkedIn.
- Approved founding year and funding stage.
- A specific newsworthy November announcement.
- Travel/pass budget.

Early deadline: September 13, 2026; $105 non-refundable fee.

Regular deadline: November 13, 2026; $225 non-refundable fee.

Event: March 16–17, 2027, Austin.

Recommended category: Entertainment, Media, Sports & Digital Platforms. Secondary option: Life Sciences, Healthcare & AssistiveTech.

- Founders must retain ownership.
- Company must not have raised more than $10 million.
- Company must be legally registered and compliant.
- One product/service per company.
- Prior SXSW Pitch participants are ineligible.
- SpotLift appears to fit the product-age rule, but legal registration must be confirmed.

Note: one cart page contains contradictory “before January 1, 2024” language. This appears inconsistent with the official eligibility support page and the purpose of the competition. Confirm with SXSW before paying if any doubt remains.

- Company description: 500 characters.
- Company/contact name, email, address, phones, and website.

Product:

- Product name.
- Elevator pitch: 2,000 characters.
- Development stage.
- Up to two demo URLs with optional credentials and viewing instructions.
- Market: 200 characters.
- Problem: 200 characters.
- Solution: 200 characters.
- Competitive advantage: 200 characters.
- Success metrics: 200 characters.
- Revenue model: 200 characters.
- Up to three competitors, each with company, website, and 100-character differentiation.
- Product launch month/year.
- Whether launching at SXSW.

Events:

- Prior showcases, awards, dates, and 100-character additional note.

Funding and traction:

- Funding to date and source type.
- Number of rounds.
- Most recent round.
- Expected fundraising before SXSW: 75 words.
- Revenue to date.
- Paying customers/users.
- Active users.
- Customer interviews.

Team:

- Up to four management-team members, each with name, title, 200-character role, and 200-character background.

Terms:

- The entry is final after submission and cannot be edited.
- Fee is non-refundable.

## 6. Web Summit Startup Programme and PITCH

Startup programme: https://websummit.com/startups/

PITCH: https://websummit.com/startups/pitch/

Event: November 9–12, 2026, Lisbon.

### Eligibility and process

- A startup must first be accepted into and purchase the Web Summit startup programme.
- ALPHA generally covers pre-investment companies or those below $1 million raised.
- Public eligibility references require a live product, working website, unique software or connected hardware, and a company less than six years old.
- PITCH is open to participating ALPHA and BETA startups with less than €5 million in funding and no discernible business-model change in the prior three years.
- Web Summit selects 105 PITCH participants.
- The startup programme includes three tickets, investor meetings, and access to apply for activations.
- The initial public form collects a business email, after which a startup specialist contacts the applicant. Full activation questions are only available after acceptance.

### Selection factors

- Growth potential and competitive environment.
- Sector growth and global scalability.
- Relevance and potential impact at Web Summit.
- Product maturity and team quality.

### SpotLift blockers

- Full participation price is not presented on the public application page and must be obtained before approval.
- Lisbon travel and accommodation budget.
- Initial application requires transmission of a business email.
- PITCH cannot be applied to directly before startup-program acceptance.

Showcase: November 10, 2026.

Invite-only prize competition: November 11, 2026.

### Important limitations
