## Files

- [requirements.md](requirements.md): eligibility, deadlines, costs, materials, and exact form questions.
- [master-application.md](master-application.md): reusable SpotLift facts and narrative blocks.
- [drafts.md](drafts.md): competition-specific draft answers and review notes.
- [founder-input-needed.md](founder-input-needed.md): facts only the founder can approve or provide.
- [submission-log.md](submission-log.md): completed submissions, confirmation details, and material assumptions.

## Review gates

1. Founder approves factual record, positioning, revenue model, and traction.
2. Founder approves each competition-specific draft.
3. Founder approves any deck, demo link, or video before it is shared.
4. Founder separately authorizes each final submission, fee, registration, or contact.

No form should be submitted from a draft containing brackets such as `[FOUNDER INPUT]`, `[VERIFY]`, or `[DECISION]`.
