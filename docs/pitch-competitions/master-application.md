## Elevator pitch

Millions of people enter gyms surrounded by equipment they do not recognize and are embarrassed to ask about. Existing workout apps assume users already know which exercise to choose and how the machine works. SpotLift closes that gap. A user points their phone at a machine, and SpotLift identifies it, explains the muscles it trains, provides step-by-step instructions and safety guidance, and helps turn that discovery into a workout. The product is live on iOS and designed for the moment when a beginner needs help most: standing in front of unfamiliar equipment on the gym floor.

## Problem

Gyms sell access to equipment, but access does not provide understanding. Beginners commonly face three connected barriers:

1. They cannot identify unfamiliar machines or exercises.
2. They are uncertain about setup, form, starting weight, and safety.
3. They feel uncomfortable interrupting staff or other members to ask for help.

Most fitness apps begin after this moment by asking users to log an exercise or follow a plan. Generic visual-search and chatbot tools can attempt identification, but they do not provide a purpose-built, safety-conscious gym workflow.

## Solution

SpotLift combines equipment recognition, a structured fitness knowledge base, and an AI training experience. In a single flow, it can identify equipment, explain what it does, show target muscles, provide tutorials and safety tips, recommend an appropriate starting approach, and connect the user to a training plan.

## Differentiation

- Starts with the physical environment through the camera instead of assuming equipment knowledge.
- Connects recognition to action: identify, learn, train, and progress.
- Uses structured, equipment-specific guidance rather than a generic visual-search result.
- Designed around beginner confidence and safety.
- Supports both English and Spanish.
- Works as a consumer product today and may support gym/member-onboarding partnerships in the future. `[DECISION: B2B ROADMAP]`

## Competitor map

### Workout programming and logging

- Fitbod
- JEFIT
- Strong
- Ladder
- Nike Training Club
- Freeletics

Difference: these products primarily plan, coach, or log workouts; equipment identification is not the central entry point.

### General visual search and AI

- Google Lens
- ChatGPT and other multimodal assistants

Difference: broad tools are not built around a structured equipment catalog, gym-specific safety workflow, workout continuity, and progress tracking.

### Gym-specific content

- Machine QR codes, manufacturer tutorials, gym-staff orientations, and YouTube searches

Difference: these experiences are fragmented, vary by facility, and often require users to know what to search for.
