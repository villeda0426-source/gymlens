## TechRise

### Company description

SpotLift is an AI gym coach for people who feel lost around unfamiliar equipment. Users point their phone at a machine to identify it, learn which muscles it trains, follow step-by-step and safety guidance, and connect that moment to a personalized workout.

### What concrete problem does the business solve?

Gym access does not give beginners the knowledge or confidence to use unfamiliar equipment. Many members cannot identify machines, do not know how to set them up safely, and feel uncomfortable asking for help. Existing fitness apps usually assume the user already knows what exercise to perform, while general visual-search tools do not provide a purpose-built training and safety workflow.

### Solution and uniqueness

SpotLift turns the phone camera into a gym onboarding tool. It identifies equipment and immediately connects the result to target-muscle diagrams, tutorials, safety tips, videos, starting guidance, saved history, and an AI-generated training plan. The differentiation is the complete identify-to-workout workflow: not just recognizing an object or logging a workout, but helping a beginner understand and use what is physically in front of them.

### Target market and acquisition

The first target is adults beginning or restarting independent gym training, especially members of commercial gyms with large equipment floors. SpotLift will acquire users through App Store search, short-form demonstrations built around “What is this machine?” moments, fitness-creator partnerships, referrals from shareable equipment guides, and gym pilots that place the product in front of new members during onboarding.

### Competitors

Fitbod, JEFIT, Strong, Ladder, Nike Training Club, Freeletics, Google Lens, ChatGPT, YouTube tutorials, gym orientations, and equipment QR-code systems. Workout apps focus on programming and logging; general AI and search tools are broad; gym content is fragmented. SpotLift begins with camera recognition and connects it to structured, safety-conscious training.
