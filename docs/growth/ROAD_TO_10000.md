# Coachlift Road to 10,000

This execution plan sits downstream of the detailed [video source blueprint](./VIDEO_SOURCE_BLUEPRINT.md) and the phased [AI-assisted marketing system](./AI_MARKETING_SYSTEM.md). It should not be activated until the foundation and approval gates in those documents are agreed.

## North Star

Reach 10,000 qualified iOS downloads while building a repeatable acquisition system that attracts gym beginners, gets them to complete a useful machine scan, and gives Coachlift enough feedback and retention data to improve the product.

The public story is **Road to 10,000**. The internal goal is not downloads alone:

- **Download:** installs Coachlift.
- **Activated user:** completes a first successful machine scan and views the guidance.
- **Retained user:** returns and completes another scan within 7 days.
- **Qualified user:** activated user who matches the beginner/returning-gym-goer audience.

Primary score: qualified activated users. Public milestone: 10,000 downloads.

## The Growth Loop

This plan adapts Steven Cravotta's four-part system from *How To Reach 10,000 App Users (Beginner Marketing)*:

1. Find the viral message: sell the transformation, not a feature list.
2. Research winning content: study formats, hooks, pains, and trends already earning attention.
3. Increase creative volume: founder content first, then creators once messages begin to work.
4. Scale proven winners: use paid ads only after organic content demonstrates attention and install intent.

The loop never ends: **research -> create -> publish -> measure -> improve -> scale**.

## Coachlift's Viral Message

### Core transformation

**From:** standing in front of an unfamiliar gym machine, embarrassed and unsure.

**To:** knowing what it is, what it trains, how to set it up, and how to start—with confidence in seconds.

### Primary promise

**Walk into any gym and know exactly what to do.**

### Repeatable message angles

1. **Gym confusion:** "POV: everyone looks like they know what this machine does except you."
2. **Machine reveal:** "This machine isn't for what most beginners think."
3. **First-90-days confidence:** make the gym easier before frustration becomes quitting.
4. **Trainer alternative:** get immediate basic machine guidance without waiting for help.
5. **Founder journey:** "I built the app I needed when the gym felt like a test."

Content should give the viewer a useful or emotionally recognizable moment first. Coachlift appears briefly as the resolution. The social post's job is to earn the next step—an App Store visit—not explain the whole product.

## Funnel and Measurement

| Stage | Job | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| Short-form content | Stop and hold the right viewer | 3-second hold, average watch time, completion, shares/saves |
| Profile/CTA | Create enough curiosity to act | Profile visits and link clicks |
| App Store listing | Convert interest into installs | Product-page conversion rate and downloads |
| Onboarding | Deliver the promised first win | First successful scan / activation rate |
| Product | Create repeat use | Day-1 and Day-7 retention, scans per activated user |
| Monetization | Establish sustainable acquisition | Trial/pay conversion, realized LTV per download |

Every link and campaign should use a unique attribution link or campaign code. Record weekly metrics by creative, hook, format, creator, and platform.

## Milestones

These stages are governed by evidence rather than fixed dates. The initial operating window is 12 weeks; the 10,000-download date will be forecast after four weeks of real conversion data.

### Stage 0 — Foundation (Week 1)

- Capture current download, activation, retention, and App Store conversion baselines.
- Confirm analytics can identify install source where platform privacy permits.
- Review the App Store page against the message "Walk into any gym and know exactly what to do."
- Create a simple weekly growth dashboard.
- Build a swipe file of at least 50 proven posts in the gym-beginner, machine-tutorial, gym-anxiety, fitness-app, and founder-building niches.
- Define the first 12 hooks across the five message angles.

Exit condition: every post can be connected to App Store visits, downloads, and activation as reliably as available tooling permits.

### Stage 1 — First 100 Qualified Users (Weeks 1–2)

- Publish founder-led content at the current sustainable cadence: 3 reels and 3 feed posts per week.
- Cross-post suitable vertical videos to Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.
- Use one clear CTA: "Download Coachlift free on iPhone."
- Share the founder story and ask for feedback in relevant communities, respecting each community's self-promotion rules.
- Personally interview or message at least 10 activated users.
- Test three message families: confusion, machine education, and founder story.

Exit condition: at least one message produces qualified installs and users can clearly repeat Coachlift's value in their own words.

### Stage 2 — First 1,000 Downloads (Weeks 3–6)

- Turn the best message into 5–10 variations instead of constantly inventing unrelated ideas.
- Increase toward one short-form video per day only when production is sustainable.
- Add fast trend-response content when a trend naturally fits the beginner-gym problem.
- Recruit 5–10 small fitness, beginner-journey, campus, or lifestyle creators for controlled tests.
- Give creators the problem, audience, proof points, examples, and claim boundaries; preserve their natural voice.
- Secure written usage rights before reusing creator footage or running ads.
- Improve the App Store page using the language and objections heard from real users.

Exit condition: at least three repeatable creative concepts generate installs, and at least one creator produces acceptable cost and quality.

### Stage 3 — Repeatable Growth (1,000–5,000 Downloads)

- Maintain an 80/20 portfolio: 80% iterations of proven concepts, 20% new experiments.
- Publish through founder, brand, and creator accounts rather than depending on one audience.
- Produce multiple openings for every winning video; the hook is the highest-leverage variable.
- Create retargetable asset families: machine reveals, confusion-to-confidence demos, mistakes/checklists, and founder proof.
- Begin small paid tests only on organic winners.
- Optimize paid activity to activated users where possible, not cheapest installs.

Exit condition: one or more channels acquire activated users predictably and creative performance can be reproduced, not merely attributed to one viral outlier.

### Stage 4 — Scale to 10,000 Downloads

- Increase creator count and paid budget gradually while activation and retention remain healthy.
- Refresh creative weekly to prevent fatigue.
- Scale budget only when cost per activated user is inside the approved economic limit.
- Add referral, testimonial, gym-partnership, and campus-ambassador experiments.
- Publish milestone updates as the Road to 10,000 story: lessons, failures, user feedback, and progress—not just celebratory counters.

Exit condition: 10,000 cumulative qualified iOS downloads, reported with activation and retention alongside the headline number.

## Weekly Content System

### Minimum sustainable cadence

- Monday: pain-led reel—gym confusion or intimidation.
- Tuesday: saveable machine guide carousel.
- Wednesday: trend/remix or machine reveal reel.
- Thursday: founder journey, user learning, or product proof post.
- Friday: confusion-to-confidence app demo reel.
- Sunday: weekly Road to 10,000 update or useful roundup.

Every reel includes a first-two-second hook, captions, a real machine or recognizable gym moment, a short Coachlift resolution, and one CTA.

### Weekly experimentation rule

- One audience problem to learn about.
- Three hook variants around the same concept.
- One new format or trend test.
- One explicit App Store conversion test.
- One product/onboarding improvement based on acquired-user feedback.

## Creative Scorecard

Review each post after 24 hours and again after 7 days:

- correct audience reached
- first-3-second hold
- completion/watch time
- shares and saves
- comments showing recognition or intent
- profile visits / link clicks
- App Store page views
- downloads
- activated users
- cost per activated user, if paid

Label each concept **Kill**, **Learn**, **Iterate**, or **Scale**. A high-view post without qualified actions is a learning asset, not automatically a winner.

## Creator and Paid-Spend Rules

- Founder-created content establishes the message before meaningful creator spend.
- Start with small creator tests; do not commit a large budget based on follower count.
- Evaluate creators on qualified views, believable delivery, installs, activation, and reusable rights.
- A results-based marketplace can be tested, but platform claims and creator economics must be independently verified.
- Never assume a video's organic popularity guarantees profitable acquisition.
- Do not scale paid ads until install attribution, activation, and a conservative LTV or approved learning budget exist.
- With a free beta and incomplete monetization data, paid ads are a capped learning expense—not yet a profit engine.

## First Seven Days

### Day 1

- Record current App Store downloads, product-page views, conversion, active devices, and retention.
- Confirm the exact definition and starting count for the public Road to 10,000 counter.

### Day 2

- Audit the App Store title, subtitle, first three screenshots, preview video, and CTA against the transformation message.

### Day 3

- Research and tag 50 successful content examples by hook, pain, format, length, proof, CTA, and engagement quality.

### Day 4

- Write and film three variants of the first concept: **"POV: you don't know what this machine does."**

### Day 5

- Edit, caption, create covers, and prepare cross-platform versions.

### Day 6

- Publish the strongest version and respond to every substantive comment.
- Share a value-first founder post in one suitable community after checking its rules.

### Day 7

- Review early signals, document user language, select the next iteration, and prepare the six-item weekly approval queue.

## Decisions Needed Before Scaling

- Starting download and activated-user counts.
- Analytics and attribution tools currently installed.
- Monthly test budget for creators and paid distribution.
- Adan's realistic weekly filming time.
- Whether the public counter represents downloads, accounts, activated users, or another clearly named metric.
