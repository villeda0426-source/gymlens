# Coachlift AI-Assisted Marketing System

## Purpose

Build a human-directed AI operating system that continuously turns market evidence into Coachlift content, creator experiments, distribution decisions, and measurable product learning on the road to 10,000 downloads.

This is **AI-assisted**, not autonomous marketing. AI researches, organizes, drafts, scores, packages, and analyzes. Adan approves positioning, claims, creative, creator commitments, spend, community posts, and publishing.

## System Loop

**Observe -> Hypothesize -> Create -> Approve -> Publish -> Measure -> Learn -> Scale**

Every output must be traceable to:

- a target audience
- a known problem or desire
- a source pattern or original hypothesis
- a funnel stage
- a success metric
- a human owner
- an approval state
- a result after publishing

## Persistent System Memory

The system needs six linked libraries:

1. **Product truth:** approved Coachlift features, claims, limitations, prices, platforms, brand rules, and App Store assets.
2. **Audience language:** exact pains, questions, objections, desires, and phrases from comments, interviews, reviews, search, and support.
3. **Pattern library:** researched content tagged by hook, format, visual device, length, proof, CTA, platform, and adaptation idea.
4. **Creative library:** every Coachlift concept, script, asset, variant, creator, rights status, publish record, and performance result.
5. **Experiment ledger:** hypothesis, variable, control, sample window, decision rule, result, learning, and next action.
6. **Funnel dashboard:** views, attention quality, profile/link action, App Store visits, downloads, activation, retention, revenue, and cost.

The system should never rely on chat history as its only memory.

## Phase 0 — Truth, Safety, and Measurement Foundation

### Goal

Make it impossible for the system to market the wrong product, fabricate a claim, or optimize against an undefined result.

### AI instructions

- Read approved Coachlift product and brand sources before producing public copy.
- Classify every proposed claim as verified, needs evidence, prohibited, or subjective creative language.
- Maintain a canonical metric dictionary.
- Map the funnel events from impression through retained user.
- Identify missing analytics or attribution rather than inventing data.
- Draft App Store and onboarding audit findings, but do not change production assets without approval.

### Human decisions

- Define whether “10,000” publicly means downloads, accounts, or activated users.
- Approve health/safety claim boundaries.
- Approve analytics changes and privacy treatment.
- Approve the initial creator and paid-learning budgets.

### Required outputs

- product-truth file
- claim registry
- metric dictionary
- event map
- baseline dashboard
- source and campaign naming convention
- approval matrix

### Exit gate

We can attribute every published item to a campaign and can measure downloads plus first successful scans at minimum.

## Phase 1 — Research Engine

### Goal

Understand what Coachlift’s intended users already watch, ask, fear, save, share, and download.

### AI instructions

- Search the approved keyword watchlist across relevant platforms and the web.
- Capture public source links and observable metrics with dates.
- Extract patterns rather than copying protected creative.
- Tag each source by audience, pain/desire, hook, format, visual mechanism, duration, proof, CTA, comments, and funnel stage.
- Separate observed evidence from inference.
- Rank opportunities on relevance, urgency, production ease, download potential, and brand fit.
- Detect duplicates and decaying trends.
- Produce a short daily brief and a deeper weekly synthesis.

### Human decisions

- Confirm which audience and problem deserve the next week’s creative capacity.
- Reject patterns that feel off-brand or exploitative.

### Required outputs

- tagged swipe file
- audience-language bank
- competitor/message map
- trend watchlist
- weekly top-five opportunity brief

### Exit gate

At least 50 tagged references and three evidence-backed message hypotheses exist for Coachlift.

## Phase 2 — Viral Message Laboratory

### Goal

Find the transformation-led messages that attract qualified gym beginners before scaling content volume.

### AI instructions

- Convert each approved research pattern into an original Coachlift hypothesis.
- Keep the user’s problem or aspiration as the subject; use Coachlift as the resolution.
- Generate several hooks around one core idea rather than unrelated one-off ideas.
- State the specific variable being tested: hook, visual, proof, CTA, length, presenter, or audience.
- Predict the desired behavior for one funnel step only.
- Screen every concept against product truth, safety, shame-free tone, and platform fit.
- Prepare an approval card with source inspiration, adaptation rationale, exact copy, shot list, CTA, and success rule.

### Human decisions

- Approve, revise, or hold every public concept.
- Choose what Adan will film personally.

### Initial Coachlift message families

- confusion -> clarity
- embarrassment -> confidence
- unknown machine -> instant explanation
- beginner mistake -> simple setup knowledge
- founder problem -> product solution

### Required outputs

- message map
- hook bank
- experiment cards
- six-item weekly approval queue

### Exit gate

At least one message family repeatedly produces qualified intent signals and activated users—not merely one high-view outlier.

## Phase 3 — Creative Production Engine

### Goal

Turn approved hypotheses into consistent, platform-native assets without losing human credibility.

### AI instructions

- Produce scripts, shot lists, app-demo timings, captions, covers, subtitles, alt text, and platform variants.
- Preserve native pacing and minimize product exposition.
- Require the product reveal to resolve the opening tension.
- Create multiple opening hooks and, when feasible, one controlled alternate CTA.
- Maintain version IDs so each asset maps to its hypothesis and results.
- Perform preflight checks for aspect ratio, safe zones, captions, audio rights, claim safety, CTA, and App Store accuracy.
- Package assets for human approval; do not auto-publish.

### Human decisions

- Record authentic founder/gym footage.
- Approve final edit, caption, destination, and schedule.

### Required outputs

- master video and platform variants
- production checklist
- rights/source record
- approval preview
- publish package

### Exit gate

The team can sustainably ship the chosen cadence with complete tracking and no skipped approvals.

## Phase 4 — Creator Experiment Engine

### Goal

Expand creative volume and audience access after Coachlift has credible message hypotheses.

### AI instructions

- Discover creators using audience fit, delivery style, comment quality, geography, and past branded-content credibility—not follower count alone.
- Draft personalized outreach and creator briefs.
- Include the audience problem, desired transformation, non-negotiable product truths, banned claims, reference structures, deliverables, deadlines, and disclosure requirements.
- Track bid, expected deliverables, usage rights, edit rights, whitelisting/Spark authorization, exclusivity, music rights, and payment structure.
- Score submitted videos before recommending acceptance.
- Never accept a bid, sign terms, send payment, or authorize ads without human approval.

### Human decisions

- Select creators and approve commercial terms.
- Approve submitted content and payment.
- Approve any ad authorization or reuse.

### Required outputs

- creator database
- outreach queue
- brief templates
- bid comparison
- rights ledger
- submission scorecards

### Exit gate

At least two creators or creator archetypes generate qualified results that can be tested again.

## Phase 5 — Distribution and Experiment Control

### Goal

Publish consistently, preserve experimental clarity, and learn from the entire funnel.

### AI instructions

- Build a weekly calendar that balances proven iterations and new tests.
- Assign a campaign ID and attributed link to every publish package.
- Draft value-first community contributions and check current community rules before approval.
- Collect performance at consistent windows such as 24 hours and 7 days.
- Compare results to relevant platform/account baselines, not universal vanity benchmarks.
- Label outcomes Kill, Learn, Iterate, or Scale with written reasoning.
- Diagnose the failing funnel transition before proposing more content.
- Record negative and null results; never erase losing tests from memory.

### Human decisions

- Approve publishing and community participation.
- Participate personally in comments and founder conversations.
- Approve the weekly learning decision.

### Required outputs

- content calendar
- approval queue
- community-post queue
- 24-hour report
- 7-day experiment report
- next-iteration recommendations

### Exit gate

The team can connect individual creatives to downstream activity reliably enough to distinguish attention from acquisition.

## Phase 6 — Paid Amplification Engine

### Goal

Use paid media to amplify proven creative while protecting Coachlift’s budget and learning quality.

### AI instructions

- Nominate ads using both organic attention quality and downstream conversion evidence.
- Require a test hypothesis, audience, budget cap, stop rule, and success rule.
- Use a conservative acquisition ceiling based on mature value data or an explicitly approved learning budget.
- Monitor cost per App Store visit, download, activated user, and retained user when available.
- Flag fatigue, audience saturation, attribution gaps, and deteriorating activation.
- Recommend budget changes; never change spend automatically.
- Keep gross revenue, net revenue, proceeds, and customer value definitions separate.

### Human decisions

- Approve every campaign launch and budget change.
- Approve use of creator identity or Spark authorization.
- Decide whether a campaign is a learning expense or profit-seeking acquisition.

### Required outputs

- paid-test brief
- creative nomination report
- budget/stop-rule sheet
- daily exception alert
- cohort outcome report

### Exit gate

Paid acquisition produces activated users within the approved ceiling without degrading retention or brand quality.

## Phase 7 — Growth Memory and Scaling

### Goal

Make each week smarter than the last and prevent the organization from relearning the same lessons.

### AI instructions

- Update the pattern and creative libraries with actual results.
- Summarize what worked, for whom, on which platform, and at which funnel stage.
- Separate durable message learning from temporary trend lift.
- Identify creative fatigue and schedule refreshes.
- Forecast the road to 10,000 as a range using recent qualified acquisition rates.
- Recommend the next bottleneck to solve: reach, click-through, App Store conversion, activation, retention, or monetization.
- Produce a weekly executive memo with evidence, uncertainty, and required decisions.

### Human decisions

- Approve strategy changes and resource shifts.
- Decide which lessons become permanent operating rules.

### Required outputs

- weekly learning memo
- updated playbook
- milestone forecast
- bottleneck analysis
- Road to 10,000 public progress draft

## Specialist Roles

| Role | Responsibility | Never does autonomously |
|---|---|---|
| Reacher | Current research, sources, trends, audience language, competitor patterns | Copy creative or declare a trend without evidence |
| Mark | Campaign direction, calendar, approvals, funnel coherence | Publish, spend, or change claims without approval |
| TrendCut | Reel concepts, scripts, shot lists, edits, trend adaptation | Use unlicensed assets or force an irrelevant trend |
| PostForge | Carousels, static posts, captions, design-ready packages | Finalize unapproved public claims |
| GrowthPulse | Measurement, experiment interpretation, learning memory | Treat views as business success or hide null results |

## Approval Matrix

| Action | AI may prepare | Human approval required |
|---|---:|---:|
| Research and tagging | Yes | No |
| Draft concepts and scripts | Yes | Before production/public use |
| Generate internal mockups | Yes | Before public use |
| Publish social content | No | Always |
| Post or comment in communities | No | Always |
| Contact creators | Draft only | Before sending |
| Accept creator bid or pay | No | Always |
| Launch or modify ads | No | Always |
| Change App Store assets | Draft only | Always |
| Change product/onboarding | Draft/spec only | Always |

## System Build Order

1. Create the structured libraries and metric dictionary.
2. Connect existing Coachlift product truth and brand assets.
3. Build research intake and tagging.
4. Build concept generation plus claim/brand checks.
5. Build the approval queue.
6. Build production packages.
7. Add publishing handoff and attribution links.
8. Add performance ingestion and experiment analysis.
9. Add creator CRM and rights ledger.
10. Add paid-test recommendations only after the measurement layer is reliable.

The first software deliverable should be a transparent system of record and approval queue—not a fully autonomous posting bot.
