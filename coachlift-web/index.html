How it works

Three steps to gym confidence

From “Where do I start?” to your last logged set, SpotLift gives your workout a clear next step.

1
📷

Find your starting point

Scan gym equipment for instructions and muscle diagrams, or tell Coach about your goals, experience, and available equipment.

2
🧠

Choose your plan

Compare two plan options, ask for changes, and choose your fit. Already have a routine? Paste it in and review the matched exercises.

3
💪

Train, log, repeat

Follow your workout, log sets and reps, and see your personal records. Come back to your saved session and pick up where you left off.
