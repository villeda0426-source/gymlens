## Audience Recommendation

**Primary target: gym beginners, 22-35, commercial gym members in their first 30 days — specifically those who have already paid for a membership but aren't going.** Research confirms that 80%+ of people feel intimidated at the gym initially. The post-summer September re-entry window is peaking this week — this audience has signed up, may have even gone once, and then stopped because they didn't know how to navigate the floor. CoachLift is the permission structure they need to actually walk up to a machine and use it correctly. Messaging should lean into "no one taught you this" and "you're not the only one who doesn't know what that machine does."

**Secondary target: coaches and personal trainers who work with gym beginners (25-45).** This cohort acts as a referral engine: a trainer who introduces CoachLift to a client is a force multiplier with zero paid acquisition cost. Organic content showing how CoachLift makes their job easier — "my clients can practice form between our sessions" — positions the app as a professional tool, not just a consumer toy. Content framed as "what I tell my beginner clients" performs extremely well in this segment and earns trust from the beginner audience simultaneously.
