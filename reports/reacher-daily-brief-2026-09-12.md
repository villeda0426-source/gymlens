- **Lead signal today — MyFitnessPal just got dangerous for beginners**: MFP launched its 2026 Summer Release (announced Aug 25) featuring an AI Coach for Premium/Premium+ iOS users — personalized nutrition guidance, food suggestions down to the portion, meal-timing coaching, plus a new Plan tab and GLP-1 medication tracking. "5 things nobody tells you about joining a gym for the first time" carousels drive more saves than almost any other fitness content format. CoachLift has not yet executed this format.
- **GLP-1 gym-goer is an emerging niche**: MFP's Summer Release added GLP-1 tracking, signaling a real user surge of people on weight-loss medications who are now joining gyms with little equipment knowledge — a precise match for CoachLift's beginner audience.
- **Pilates surge and functional fitness are defining 2026 trends**: Pilates has seen massive TikTok/Instagram growth through 2026. Functional fitness and evidence-backed strength training are dominant over fad challenges. CoachLift's strength equipment focus is on the right side of the trend line.

## Audience Recommendation

Today's research reinforces yesterday's core audience (gym-curious 18–32 year olds in their first 60 days) but adds a new, highly specific sub-segment worth activating: **GLP-1 gym beginners**. These are people currently taking semaglutide or tirzepatide (Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound) who have lost significant weight and are now entering gyms for the first time — or returning after years away. MyFitnessPal just greenlit them with a dedicated tracking feature, which means this cohort is real, identifiable, and growing. They are often older (35–55), have higher disposable income, are highly motivated, and have zero gym experience. CoachLift's camera-scan mechanic is a perfect first step for someone who has lost 40 lbs on a GLP-1 drug and just walked into a gym for the first time in their life.

The "5 things nobody tells you" confession/identity-call format is the highest-save content format for beginners specifically. Mark should prioritize executing this carousel format this week — it beats all other formats for saves and shares among the 18–35 gym beginner cohort, and saves directly predict follower growth and download attribution. This is the most actionable format shift from today's research.

### 3 Reel Ideas

**Reel 1: "I Pretended to Check My Phone" — Confession Hook**
- **Hook** (first 3 sec): Creator stands in front of a cable machine, visibly hesitating, looks at their phone. Text overlay: *"I stood there for 3 minutes pretending to check my phone because I had no idea how to use this machine."*
- **Structure**:
  - 0–3s: The relatable confession moment — phone-faking at the machine
  - 4–9s: "I didn't want to look dumb. I didn't want to ask." — text cards, no voice needed
  - 10–18s: CoachLift scan demo — quick point, machine identified, muscle map visible, starting weight shown
  - 19–26s: Creator now using the machine confidently
  - 27–30s: End card: "You don't have to fake it. Download CoachLift — free."
- **Production notes**: Extremely low production bar. Authentic setting in a real gym. No need for a fitness model — someone who genuinely looks like a beginner is better. Keep it real, keep it under 30 seconds. Muted-friendly captions essential. This format earns saves and shares because it's emotionally true.

**Reel 2: "Pilates Person Meets the Weights Floor"**
- **Hook**: Creator (Pilates-adjacent, not a bodybuilder) stands at the edge of the weights area looking uncertain. Text: *"I've been doing Pilates for a year. The weights floor still terrifies me."*
- **Structure**:
  - 0–3s: Hook — Pilates person at the weights floor threshold
  - 4–10s: "Every machine looks like a different exam I haven't studied for."
  - 11–20s: CoachLift scan — pick 3 different machines, show it identifying each, muscle map, beginner weight range
  - 21–28s: "Turns out the weights floor isn't so scary when you know what everything does."
  - 28–30s: Download CTA
- **Production notes**: This targets the large Pilates-crossover audience that is currently platform-dominant. If creator has a Pilates background, authenticity is automatic. Trending audio from current Reels chart recommended. Keep Pilates identity visible in their aesthetic — that's the hook for this audience.

**Reel 3: "What MFP Users Are Missing" — Complementary App Angle**
- **Hook**: Split screen — MFP food log on one side, CoachLift scan on the other. Text: *"Your food app is sorted. But what about the equipment you have no idea how to use?"*
- **Structure**:
  - 0–3s: Split-screen hook (MFP / CoachLift)
  - 4–10s: "You know what to eat. But you walked past the cable machine again today."
  - 11–20s: CoachLift scan — full demo on a machine MFP users commonly see but avoid
  - 21–28s: "The two-app setup that actually makes the gym work."
  - 28–30s: "CoachLift. Free. Link in bio."
- **Production notes**: Tap into MFP's massive user base without being adversarial. "Complement, don't compete" framing. If this format lands, it could be a recurring series: "The app your favorite app doesn't replace."
