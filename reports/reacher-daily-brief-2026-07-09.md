## Audience Recommendation

CoachLift's strongest current audience signal is the **"Gym Anxious Beginner"** — primarily Gen Z and Millennial women (18-35) who have started going to the gym but feel intimidated by unfamiliar equipment and don't know how to ask for help. This group is self-identifying at scale on TikTok (#gymbeginner, #shygirlworkout, #gymxiety) and actively seeks content that gives them a private, judgment-free way to learn. Research shows Gen Z are the most likely demographic to feel anxious at the gym (62%), and notably, 77% of men and 71% of women say fitness influencers have made gym culture *worse* — making a neutral, AI-powered guidance tool especially resonant with this cohort.

A high-value secondary audience is the **"Returning Exerciser"** — typically women 25-45 who last went to the gym 1-3 years ago and feel unfamiliar with newer equipment or changed gym layouts. They have high purchase intent, strong device literacy, and are already motivated (they just need the confidence layer). Apple's "Make Your Fitness Comeback" Fitness+ program is directly recruiting this segment right now. Ladder's explosive growth (400K members, 80% women) confirms the broader women's strength market is real and growing — CoachLift should not cede this audience to competitors who don't offer equipment identification.
