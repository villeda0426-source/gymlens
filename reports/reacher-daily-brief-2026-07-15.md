- **"Gymtimidation" content is in a summer spike:** Mid-July coincides with new gym sign-ups ahead of fall routines. The emotional hook of "you just joined and feel lost" is at peak resonance RIGHT NOW. This week is the highest-priority window to push awareness content.

## Audience Recommendation

CoachLift's primary target this week is the **mid-July gym joiner: 22–35, signed up in the last 2–4 weeks, going alone, intimidated by unfamiliar machines.** This cohort is at the exact moment of highest churn risk — they've paid for a membership, walked in a few times, felt embarrassed, and are now opening their phones looking for reassurance before giving up. Research confirms 47% of non-gym members avoid gyms because of discomfort, and 32% fear negative judgment — CoachLift solves both the informational problem (what is this machine?) and the psychological one (you're allowed to not know). Content that validates the anxiety first, then delivers the tool, will outperform pure how-to content for this segment.

Secondary target: **gym-curious Instagram lurkers aged 28–44** who follow fitness accounts but haven't committed. This cohort responds to authority-signaling ("used by beginners who became regulars") and clear promises over clever hooks. A longer Reel (45–55 seconds) with a problem-first structure — naming the exact pain before revealing the solution — will perform better for this group than fast-cut stylized content. The IG Reels median viewer is 28–44, making it CoachLift's most mature and conversion-ready audience on social.
