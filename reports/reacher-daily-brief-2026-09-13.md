## Audience Recommendation

**Primary target this week: gym beginners, 18-34, first 30 days at a commercial gym.** September's "fresh start" energy means this cohort is at its annual peak — gyms are reporting new sign-ups and hybrid memberships are up 27% vs single-access. These users are not fitness-illiterate; they're *equipment-illiterate*. They know they should work out but have never been shown how to approach an unfamiliar cable machine without embarrassment. This is exactly the gap CoachLift was built for.

**Secondary target: returning gym-goers (dormant 6+ months).** This audience carries residual anxiety from a prior experience of feeling lost. They are cognitively primed to download an app that "has their back" — they've been burned before and want a safety net. Content framed as "you don't have to figure it out alone this time" will index very high with this group as a September re-entry hook.
