## Executive Read
- **Fall re-entry spike is live:** September is the second-largest gym re-enrollment month after January, and fitness content about "getting back on track" is peaking across Instagram and TikTok. CoachLift's scan-on-day-one value prop is at maximum relevance right now — this is a weekly traffic opportunity not to miss with passive content.

This is a window to capture review traffic, ASO positioning, and first-mover social mindshare before they re-emerge. Lean into "most-updated scanner app" angles now.
- **Flop-core is the week's winning gym format:** Creators posting gym failures, awkward first-rep moments, and equipment confusion are outperforming polished workout content this week. This is CoachLift's native content territory — real beginners, real confusion, real scan-and-fix moments. Produce this before the format saturates.
- **GLP-1 users are the fastest-growing new gym demographic:** Ozempic, Wegovy, and Mounjaro users are entering gyms for the first time after significant weight loss. They are often 40–60, physically unfamiliar with equipment, high-intent, and deeply underserved by beginner fitness content. CoachLift's safety-first framing maps perfectly to their anxiety profile.
- **Community training is the #1 fitness macro-trend of 2026 (Les Mills):** People are returning to gyms specifically for the social layer — solo app-only fitness is declining. CoachLift should frame gym-going as the destination, not just the equipment, and position the app as the confidence layer that gets beginners across the gym door and into that community.

## Audience Recommendation

**Primary target this week:** Fall re-starters aged 24–38 who had an active gym routine earlier in 2026 but drifted through summer. This cohort is self-aware about lapsing, slightly embarrassed, and highly motivated by "fresh start" framing. They know gyms exist and they've used equipment before — but 3–5 months away means they've forgotten machines, lost their routine, and feel the intimidation barrier again. CoachLift is the fastest way to walk back in without looking lost. Content angle: "You're not starting over. You're just a little rusty. CoachLift has you in 10 seconds."

**Secondary target:** GLP-1 medication users entering the gym for the first time. This is the fastest-growing net-new gym demographic in 2026 — confirmed by Les Mills and Gymdesk fitness industry trend reports. They are typically older (35–60), newly mobile, eager but anxious, and ignored by almost every fitness app targeting 25-year-old athletes. CoachLift's non-judgmental, scan-first interface is the safest first experience in any gym. This audience also skews toward social proof, medical authority framing, and "doctor-recommended" adjacent language. Reach them via Facebook and Instagram primary (less TikTok), and consider static post + Reel combination.
