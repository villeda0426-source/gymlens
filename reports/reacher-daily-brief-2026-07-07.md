## Audience Recommendation

CoachLift's clearest acquisition target today is **women aged 18–34 in their first 60 days at the gym**. The research is unambiguous: 65% of women report gym anxiety vs. 35% of men, and the #ShyGirlWorkout trend (200M+ TikTok views) shows this audience is actively seeking content that makes them feel capable — not coached. They are not looking for a personal trainer substitute. They are looking for a reason to stay.

The secondary target is **any new gym joiner in the 0–90 day window regardless of gender**, particularly people who joined after a life milestone (new year, new city, new relationship status). This group saves and shares content compulsively — they are in research mode, building identity around fitness before they've built habit. Content that meets them at "I don't know what this machine does" with a no-judgment, practical answer is the highest-leverage place to acquire them, because that moment happens in-app (camera scan) and on social (Reel discovery) simultaneously.

### 3 Reel Ideas

**Reel 1: The Shy Girl Scan**
- **Hook (0–3s):** Handheld, first-person POV walking into a gym. Text overlay: *"When you have no idea what you're doing but you came anyway."*
- **Structure:** 3s hook → 5s "walking past intimidating equipment" → 5s "pulls out phone, scans machine" → 10s CoachLift tutorial playing on screen → 5s person confidently doing the exercise correctly → final card: "CoachLift. Know the gym before it knows you."
- **Production notes:** Shoot in a real gym (not a studio). Natural gym sounds — no voice-over needed. Use a trending ambient or lo-fi sound from this week's TikTok audio chart. Keep it handheld and slightly imperfect — polish reads as an ad, authenticity reads as a creator. No gym-bro energy. Soft lighting preferred.

**Reel 2: "99% Use This Wrong"**
- **Hook (0–3s):** Bold text on-screen: *"99% of people use this machine wrong."* Cut immediately to a shot of the cable machine or chest press.
- **Structure:** 3s hook text → 8s showing the common wrong setup (exaggerated but relatable) → 3s "vs." transition → 8s showing the correct form (guided by CoachLift tutorial playing in PiP corner) → 5s payoff: person getting a good rep in → 5s outro: "Scan it first. CoachLift — link in bio."
- **Production notes:** PiP (picture-in-picture) of the CoachLift scan/tutorial screen is the key creative element — keep it legible. Add captions. Ideal length: 32–38 seconds. Shoot two or three machines as variants to A/B test — chest press, lat pulldown, and cable row are the highest confusion-rate machines for beginners.

**Reel 3: POV First Day**
- **Hook (0–3s):** Walking shot with text: *"POV: You've walked past the lat pulldown 6 times pretending you know what it does."*
