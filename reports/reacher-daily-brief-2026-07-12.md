### 3 Reel Ideas

**Reel 1: "Nobody Knows What This Machine Does" (The Reframe)**
- **Hook (0–3s):** Creator looks directly at camera: *"Nobody actually knows what half the machines at the gym do. They're just pretending."*
- **Structure:** Hook → 2-second cut of intimidating gym floor → "So we built this" → 6-second clean screen recording of CoachLift scan → muscle map reveal → creator smiling: *"Now you know."*
- **Production notes:** Shoot hook in gym, handheld, slight motion — feels authentic not polished. Screen record should be clean and zoomed in. Total runtime: 20–25 seconds. No talking during the scan demo — let the visual do the work. Use trending audio underneath (low instrumental, not a meme sound for this one).

**Reel 2: "POV: Scared of This Machine for 3 Weeks"**
- **Hook (0–3s):** Text overlay on black: *"POV: You've walked past this machine 12 times without touching it."* Camera cuts to a cable machine.
- **Structure:** Slow pan of the machine → phone comes into frame → scan starts → tutorial appears on screen (muscle map + instructions visible) → creator uses machine correctly → final frame: *"Day 1 took 30 seconds."*
- **Production notes:** Shoot in portrait, POV-ish angle. The scan moment is the emotional payoff — hold on it for 3–4 seconds. Voiceover optional ("here's what happens when you scan it"). End card with app name and download prompt. 15–22 seconds total.

**Reel 3: "The Gym Anxiety Confession" (Before State)**
- **Hook (0–3s):** Creator talking to camera, gym background: *"I used to walk into the gym and go straight to the treadmill. Not because I liked cardio. Because I had no idea what anything else did."*
- **Structure:** Confession (5s) → "Then I found this" (1s) → scan demo on weight machine (6s) → "Now I actually use the gym" (3s) → CTA card
- **Production notes:** Authentic, low-fi tone — no ring light, messy gym background is fine. Creator should be relatable, not a fitness model. If creator is not available, this works as a text-on-screen Reel with B-roll. 20–30 seconds.
