## Audience Recommendation

CoachLift's strongest audience signal today is the **"Celebrating Baby Gymmer"** — the Gen Z and younger Millennial beginner (18-30) who has just started going to the gym and is publicly documenting their early, imperfect progress under trending hashtags like #babymuscles, #gymtok, and #gymgirlsoftiktok. This cohort is already sharing their beginner moments on social media and searching for validation and guidance — not expert-level instruction. They want to feel smart, not coached. CoachLift's scan-first experience delivers exactly that: private confidence without judgment. The content hook is celebration, not education.

A high-value secondary target remains the **"Gym Plan Without a Map" beginner** — the Apple Fitness+ user who has just completed a "Strength Basics in 3 Weeks" program and walked into a gym with a plan but no equipment literacy. This user already trusts AI guidance (Apple's Workout Buddy), has a plan in hand from Ladder or Apple, and is now standing in front of an unfamiliar piece of equipment. CoachLift is the missing last mile. The positioning is clear: "You've got the plan. CoachLift shows you what to do when you get there."
