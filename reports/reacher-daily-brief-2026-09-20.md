## Audience Recommendation

**Primary: Day 18-22 gym beginners — today is the eye of the storm.** People who started the gym in the first week of September are right at the statistical peak of dropout likelihood today. Sundays are when they mentally negotiate whether to continue. The content most likely to convert is validation-first — acknowledging that week three of a new gym habit feels exactly like this, that it hasn't clicked yet, and that there is a specific, concrete thing that helps (scanning machines instead of guessing). The CTA should be low-friction: "Download free, try it Monday morning." Urgency belongs in the timing ("this is the week most people quit") not in the product pitch.

**Secondary: Returning October gym-goers.** Eleven days out from October 1, a second audience is warming up: people who had a gym habit in spring, let it slide over summer, and are looking for a clean-slate moment to come back. This audience isn't anxious in the way beginners are — they're lapsed, not new — but they share the "equipment feels intimidating after a break" experience. A single piece of content this weekend seeding CoachLift as the "reset tool" (as opposed to "beginner tool") opens a new acquisition lane and differentiates against competitors who don't serve the returning-lapsed cohort.

## Content Handoff For Mark

### 3 Reel Ideas

**Reel 1: The Before/After Reveal**
- **Hook:** *"I walked up to this machine with zero idea what it was. 10 seconds later:"* — cut immediately to completed set with perfect form. No intro, no preamble. The visual contrast IS the hook.
- **Structure:** (0-2s) Wide shot: person standing in front of unfamiliar machine, looking at it uncertainly. Text on screen: "Me. Zero idea." (2-8s) Close-up of phone with CoachLift scan in progress. UI pops — machine ID, muscle map, tutorial. (8-20s) Same person executing the movement with correct form and confidence. (20-28s) Hold on the confident face. CoachLift logo. "Scan any machine. Know exactly what to do."
- **Production Notes:** Shoot on a real commercial gym floor. The cut at 2s needs to be clean and fast — the abruptness is the hook. Use the CoachLift scan screen recording as the pivot. Don't over-explain; the visual story carries itself. Ideal length: 28-32 seconds. No voiceover needed — let the format speak.

**Reel 2: Machine Challenge Episode 2 — "Machine 2 of 5"**
- **Hook:** *"Machine 2 of 5. Day 20 at the gym. I had absolutely no idea what this was."* — on-screen text over a shot approaching the cable row machine.
- **Structure:** (0-4s) Title card: "Machine 2 of 5. Day 20." Walking toward cable row, looking uncertain. (4-12s) CoachLift scan. UI shows: cable row, back muscles highlighted, form guide, starting weight. (12-24s) Execute the set. Show the form — shoulders back, controlled movement. (24-32s) "2 down. 3 to go. Machine 3 drops Wednesday." Follow CTA on screen.
- **Production Notes:** Match the editing style and framing from Episode 1 for series consistency. The cable row is ideal for Episode 2 — visible machine, clear muscle group, satisfying form to watch. Keep the uncertainty authentic at the approach — don't over-act it. The CoachLift screen time can be shorter in Episode 2 now that viewers have seen it once.

**Reel 3: "Apple Is Building This — But It's Free Right Now"**
- **Hook:** *"Apple just announced an AI gym coach. It's not out yet. This one is — and it's free."* — talking head, direct to camera, low-key delivery.
- **Structure:** (0-5s) Hook delivery: direct, calm. Not hype — matter-of-fact. (5-15s) "Here's what CoachLift does right now:" Quick product demo — scan, muscle map, starting weight guidance, form tutorial. All in CoachLift. (15-25s) "You already have a gym membership. The AI coach for the floor is already here." CoachLift icon on App Store. (25-30s) "Free. Download before Monday." 
- **Production Notes:** This is the one Reel that can be shot anywhere — no gym location required. Keep the Apple reference factual and calm (they did announce this). Don't be dismissive of Apple — be confident. The contrast sells itself. Clean background, good lighting, 30-second target.
